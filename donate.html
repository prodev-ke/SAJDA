Where Your Contribution Goes

Three destinations. Nothing else.

Contributions to this appeal are applied to the project described on this site, and are accounted for by the organising committee.

Construction Building the permanent Sagante Jaldessa Islamic Education Centre — the campus that replaces mabati structures and borrowed mosque spaces.
The 8 Centres Strengthening the infrastructure of the eight madrasa centres already serving children across Sagante Jaldesa Ward today.
The Waqf The KSH 80,000,000 endowment — a five-storey rental building whose income pays teachers' salaries, administration and maintenance permanently.
